Corn Bread

Description

Corn bread, what's not to like? Yields 9 servings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup corn meal
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2-4 Tbsp. sugar
  • 4 tsp. baking powder
  • ½ tsp. salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup veg. oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°.
  2. Mix all dry ingredients.
  3. Add liquid ingredients to dry ingredients.
  4. Beat until smooth.
  5. Bake in 8” square pan for 20-23 minutes.
