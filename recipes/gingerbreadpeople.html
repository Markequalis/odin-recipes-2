Gingerbread People

Description

Tasty gingerbread cookies. What do you usually go for first? I break off the arms!

Ingredients

  • 1 small box of cook and serve butterscotch pudding mix
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • 1½ cup flour
  • 1 tsp. ginger
  • ½ tsp. cinnamon
  • ½ tsp. baking soda
  • Decorations or icing
