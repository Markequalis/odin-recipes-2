Gingerbread People

Description

Tasty gingerbread cookies. What do you usually go for first? I break off the arms! Makes 12 large cookies or many smaller ones depending on size of cookie cutter used.

Ingredients

  • 1 small box of cook and serve butterscotch pudding mix
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1½ cup flour
  • 1 tsp. ginger
  • ½ tsp. cinnamon
  • ½ tsp. baking soda
  • Decorations or icing

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°.
  2. Cream pudding mix, butter, and brown sugar in a mixing bowl.
  3. Beat in egg.
  4. Add dry ingredients and mix until blended.
  5. Roll out on floured board and cut with cookie cutter.
  6. Add decorations as desired.
  7. Bake for ~10 minutes.
  8. Let cool on baking sheets for 5 minutes before removing to a cool rack.
  9. Add icing as desired.
